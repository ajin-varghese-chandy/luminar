Frequent Queries

Common questions from new arrivals.

Visit the Shinjuku-B Cybernetics Bureau (Level 3). Bring your ID chip, ¥5,000 processing fee, and pass the neural aptitude test. Processing takes 24-48 hours.

The Spire requires Elite access tier. Eden-Dome is open to all but enforces strict no-chrome policies. Shibuya-Ω is fully public.

Personal drones are allowed only in Shibuya-Ω and Eden-Dome (eco mode). Shinjuku-B has a no-fly zone. The Spire automatically grounds unauthorized UAVs.

Neo-Tokyo uses digital Yen (¥). All transactions are processed through the city's quantum ledger. Physical cash is obsolete. Crypto is accepted in Shinjuku-B.
