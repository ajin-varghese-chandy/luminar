About Eduardo

Early Life

Born on March 19, 1982 in São Paulo, Brazil. His family moved to Miami, Florida after security concerns. Eduardo attended The Gulliver Schools before going to Harvard University.

Harvard & Facebook

While studying Economics at Harvard, he met Mark Zuckerberg. Together they co-founded "The Facebook" in February 2004. Eduardo served as CFO and invested the first $19,000 to get it started.

Life in Singapore

In 2011, Eduardo moved to Singapore. He built B Capital Group, a major venture capital firm. He now focuses on investing in tech startups across Southeast Asia and beyond.
